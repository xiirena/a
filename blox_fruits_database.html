Blox Fruits Comprehensive Companion

Every Fighting Style, Key Accessory, and Gun broken down by Sea, Requirements, and Buffs

⚔️ Fighting Styles

Combat First Sea
Buffs / Moves

Basic visual appearance: Bare fists. Features standard left-click punch combos, Ground Smash, and Rush moves.

How to Get & Location

Starting fighting style for all players. Cannot be re-learned if changed.

Dark Step First Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Flaming legs when using 'Overheat'. Buffs: Increases kick speed/AOE damage via skills.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $150,000. Bought from the Dark Step Teacher at Pirate Village.

Electric First Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Blue electrical sparks around hands. Features fast stuns and dashes.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $500,000. Bought from the Mad Scientist at Skylands (lower levels).

Water Kung Fu First Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Water-splash effects. Highest M1 punch speed in the First Sea.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $750,000. Bought from the Water Kung Fu Teacher at Underwater City.

Dragon Breath Second Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Purple dragon-fire breaths and explosive impacts.

How to Get & Location

Cost: 1,500 Fragments. Bought from Sabi at the Kingdom of Rose (inside the wall arches).

Superhuman Second Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: High-speed sonic impacts and wind distortions. Incredible for combos.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $3,000,000. Requires 300+ Mastery on Dark Step, Electric, Water Kung Fu, and Dragon Breath. Bought from Martial Arts Master at Snow Mountain.

Death Step Second Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Intense purple/vermilion flames on legs. Huge upgrade to Dark Step damage.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $2,500,000 + 5,000 Fragments. Requires 400 Mastery on Dark Step and Library Key drop from Awakened Ice Admiral. Bought from Phoeyu the Reformed at Ice Castle.

Sharkman Karate Second Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Giant pressure waves and massive water blasts. Extremely consistent hitboxes.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $2,500,000 + 5,000 Fragments. Requires 400 Mastery on Water Kung Fu and Water Key drop from Tide Keeper. Bought from Sharkman Teacher at Forgotten Island.

Electric Claw Third Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Red/golden lightning claws. Extemely rapid M1 click speed.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $3,000,000 + 5,000 Fragments. Requires 400 Mastery on Electric. Complete a 30-second run quest for Previous Hero at Floating Turtle.

Dragon Talon Third Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Massive fiery dragon claws and giant fire explosion fields.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $3,000,000 + 5,000 Fragments. Requires 400 Mastery on Dragon Breath and a Fire Essence from the Haunted Castle Death King gacha. Given to Uzoth at Haunted Castle.

Godhuman Third Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Golden ancient shockwaves and dynamic multi-target rushes.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $5,000,000 + 5,000 Fragments. Requires 400 Mastery on Superhuman, Death Step, Sharkman Karate, Electric Claw, and Dragon Talon + various island materials. Bought from Ancient Monk inside Floating Turtle tree.

Sanguine Art Third Sea
Buffs / Moves

Visual: Dark blood-red energy tentacles and lifesteal properties.

How to Get & Location

Cost: $5,000,000 + 5,000 Fragments. Requires Leviathan Heart, 20 Demonic Wisps, and 20 Vampire Fangs. Taught by Shafi in the Third Sea outposts.

👑 Key Accessories & Stats

Warrior Helmet First Sea
Visual Appearance

A gladiator-style spiked helmet worn over the character's head.

Exact Buffs
  • +12.5% Melee Damage
  • +12.5% Sword Damage
  • -5% Melee cooldowns
How to Get

Reward for completing the Bartilo Quest line / defeating Marine Commodore targets.

Swan Glasses Second Sea
Visual Appearance

Stylish pink-tinted flamingo sunglasses.

Exact Buffs
  • +8% Overall Damage (All types)
  • +8% Defense against all attacks
  • +8% Cooldown Reduction
  • +25% Movement Speed
  • +250 Health & Energy
How to Get

Rare 2.5% drop from defeating Don Swan at the Swan Mansion.

Pale Scarf Third Sea
Visual Appearance

A long, tattered white/cream scarf wrapped around the neck.

Exact Buffs
  • +15% Sword Damage
  • +15% Blox Fruit Damage
  • +2 Instinct Dodges
  • 10x Instinct Vision Range
How to Get

100% drop guaranteed from defeating Cake Prince or Dough King at the Sea of Treats.

Hunter's Cape Third Sea
Visual Appearance

A long fluid cape available in Black, Green, or Red variations.

Exact Buffs
  • +10% Melee, Sword, and Gun Damage
  • +80% Movement Speed
  • +750 Health
How to Get

Dropped randomly by Elite Pirates (Diablo, Deandre, Urban) via the Elite Hunter quests.

🔫 Notable Guns

Cannon First Sea
Visual & Utility

A massive handheld black cannon. Fires slow but highly explosive knockback balls.

How to Get

Purchased for $100,000 from the Advanced Weapon Dealer at Pirate Village.

Acidum Rifle Second Sea
Visual & Utility

A metallic mossy green vintage rifle. Inflicts stackable ticking acid poison damage.

How to Get

Encountered as a rare drop from the Factory Raid event at the Kingdom of Rose.

Kabucha Second Sea
Visual & Utility

A giant black multi-layered slingshot. Known for the absolute fastest gun click-rate for rapid stuns.

How to Get

Purchased from the Usopp island NPC for 1,500 Fragments after beating the raid boss.

Soul Guitar Third Sea
Visual & Utility

A skeleton-themed electric rock guitar that fires soul beams and notes. The undisputed best support gun in the game.

How to Get

Crafted at the Haunted Castle machine during a Full Moon after completing 5 structural puzzles. Costs 500 Bones, 250 Ectoplasm, 1 Dark Fragment, and 5,000 Fragments.
